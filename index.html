SeatSwap: Learning-Assisted Differentiable Kinematics for Human-Object Interaction Adaptation in Production-Scale Character Animation

Jasmine Liang, Stephen Mylabathula, Shai Segev, Priyamvad Ravindra Deshmukh, Ying Liu, and Amaury Aubel

Meta Reality Labs

📄 Paper (PDF) 🔗 DOI
⌄

SeatSwap automatically adapts motion-captured sitting animations to arbitrary 3D furniture geometry.

Abstract

Training robust motion tracking models requires massive datasets of human-object interactions across diverse body shapes, poses, and furniture geometries. Collecting such data in the real world is prohibitively expensive by using motion capture (MoCap) system: each furniture piece must be 3D-scanned, motion capture sessions require significant studio time, and the combinatorial explosion of body shapes, sitting poses, and furniture variations makes exhaustive capture impossible. We present SeatSwap, a differentiable optimization framework that solves this data scarcity problem by automatically adapting a small set of motion-captured sitting animations to arbitrary 3D furniture geometry. Given a captured sitting motion and a target furniture mesh, SeatSwap produces a physically plausible motion sequence of sitting pose where the character maintains proper contact with the seat surface—effectively enabling unlimited permutations of body-furniture combinations from limited source data. Our key technical contribution is a novel biomechanical optimization pipeline that operates directly on skeletal parameters, enabling gradient-based refinement through forward kinematics and linear blend skinning. SeatSwap retargets existing motion-captured animations while preserving their natural motion characteristics. We integrate neural signed distance fields for differentiable intersection computation and extend the system to support multi-person scenarios through sequential optimization with person-to-person collision avoidance, as well as lying down poses for bed and lounge furniture. Our system processes thousands of furniture pieces in parallel via a distributed job orchestration system, generating the diverse synthetic training data needed to train motion tracking models at Meta scale.
